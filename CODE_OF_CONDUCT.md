# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

## English

This project follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant. Participation must be welcoming, lawful, and technically constructive for people of every background and experience level.

### Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and open to correction.
- Give constructive feedback about code and ideas, not personal attacks.
- Respect different experience levels, languages, and working styles.
- Credit original authors and respect licenses, privacy, and consent.
- Keep discussion relevant to the project.

### Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or sexualized attention.
- Doxxing, impersonation, or publishing private information without consent.
- Sustained trolling, bad-faith disruption, or hostile demands.
- Uploading malicious code, unauthorized data, or content you cannot legally share.

### Scope and enforcement

These rules apply to project spaces and to public representation of the project. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content, reject contributions, suspend participation, or ban accounts when necessary to protect the community.

Use GitHub's report-abuse mechanism for harassment or sensitive reports. Non-sensitive moderation questions may be raised through a GitHub Issue. Maintainers will review reports as time and available evidence permit, while respecting the privacy and safety of everyone involved.

## 中文

本项目遵循 Contributor Covenant 的基本精神。无论背景和经验水平，所有参与者都应获得友善、合法且有助于技术协作的环境。

### 期望行为

- 尊重他人，表达具体，并愿意接受纠正。
- 针对代码和观点提出建设性意见，不攻击个人。
- 尊重不同经验水平、语言与协作方式。
- 标明原作者，遵守许可证，尊重隐私与知情同意。
- 讨论保持与项目相关。

### 不可接受的行为

- 骚扰、歧视、威胁或带有性意味的关注。
- 泄露个人信息、冒充他人或未经同意发布隐私。
- 持续挑衅、恶意破坏或敌意施压。
- 上传恶意代码、未授权数据或无权分享的内容。

### 适用范围与执行

这些规则适用于项目空间以及代表项目公开活动的情形。为保护社区，维护者可以编辑、隐藏或删除内容，拒绝贡献，暂停参与或封禁账号。

骚扰或敏感问题请使用 GitHub 的举报机制；非敏感的社区管理问题可以通过 GitHub Issue 提出。维护者会在时间和证据允许的范围内审查举报，并尽量保护相关人员的隐私与安全。
